# Operate when the network boundary is part of the architecture

A disconnected design must account for the continuing movement and validation of
trusted content, not just the first platform installation.

## Products in scope

- Red Hat OpenShift
- Red Hat Quay
- Red Hat Ansible Automation Platform and private automation hub
- Red Hat Satellite
- Red Hat OpenShift AI

## Technical focus

- Release images, Operator catalogs, and application-image mirroring
- Package, automation-content, and model-artifact promotion
- Controlled updates, exceptions, rollback, and evidence
- Certificate, registry, and repository lifecycle ownership
- Security-metadata and vulnerability-response paths

## Architecture question

For every artifact, identify the upstream source, authorized retriever, validation
step, transfer method, internal repository, promotion decision, rejection path,
and retained evidence.
