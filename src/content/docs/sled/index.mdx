# Technical guidance for public-sector platform decisions

State, local government, and education organizations often balance long-lived
applications, constrained teams, regulated data, mission continuity, and
infrastructure that cannot always depend on a public network. This section
organizes the technical library around those conditions.

## How to use this resource

1. Start with the mission or operational outcome—not a product name.
2. Identify the technical constraints, dependencies, owners, and evidence needs.
3. Review the relevant Red Hat platform guide and field notes.
4. Use a bounded lab or proof to test the highest-risk assumptions.
5. Validate the production design and supported configuration with your Red Hat
   account team and current official documentation.

## Project Lightwell
