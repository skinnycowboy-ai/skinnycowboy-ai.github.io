# State and local government field notes

State and local government environments often combine long-lived applications,
limited specialist capacity, distributed ownership, procurement constraints, and
high expectations for constituent services.

## Products in scope

- Red Hat OpenShift
- Red Hat Enterprise Linux
- Red Hat Ansible Automation Platform
- Red Hat Advanced Cluster Management for Kubernetes

## Technical focus

- Constituent-facing application delivery
- Shared platform services across agencies or departments
- Operational resilience and recovery ownership
- Repeatable infrastructure and compliance operations
- Modernization paths that preserve mission continuity

## Architecture questions

- Which service failures create the greatest mission or constituent impact?
- Which operational work depends on a small number of people?
- Where do agency boundaries complicate shared platform ownership?
- Which applications can move in phases rather than through a single cutover?
