# Education technology and research platforms

This section connects Red Hat platform capabilities to recurring education
technology patterns without assuming that every institution has the same
operating model, funding, or risk boundary.

## Products in scope

- Red Hat OpenShift
- Red Hat OpenShift AI
- Red Hat Ansible Automation Platform
- Red Hat Enterprise Linux

## Technical focus

- Shared research and AI platforms
- Student- and faculty-facing application delivery
- Campus infrastructure and network automation
- Platform engineering across distributed application teams
- Workforce, faculty, and student skills development

## Architecture questions

- Which teams need isolated environments, and which services can be shared?
- Where do sensitive research data and model artifacts need to remain?
- Who operates the platform after the initial project is complete?
- What evidence is required before a workload moves from research to production?
